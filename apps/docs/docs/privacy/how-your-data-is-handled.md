## What Waves can see

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest on our servers, and the copy on
your phone is encrypted too. That is not the same as the server being blind:
Waves stores your groups, expenses and Personal records so it can sync them, and
Waves can read them. They are never shared with other people except through the
groups you are in.

## AI features

**Ask** and the **voice assistant** are optional. To answer, Waves sends the
entries you ask about to our AI provider. They are not used for training. Before
the first time, the app asks you to allow it, once per account on that device. If
you say **Not now**, nothing is sent.

## Couple and family spaces

- Notifications for couple and family groups say who did what, such as
  "Priya added an expense in Home", and never show amounts, descriptions or
  notes on your lock screen.
- When you create one, the app offers to lock Waves with your fingerprint.
- **Only me** on an expense saves it to your private Personal records instead of
  the group. Your partner is not told and balances do not change.

## Signing out

Signing out on the web clears the copy kept in that browser. On the phone, signing
out destroys the key to the encrypted local copy.
