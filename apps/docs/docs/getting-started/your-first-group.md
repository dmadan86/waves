A group is where shared money lives. Everything else in Waves hangs off one.

## Making one

**New group** is on Home, and on the empty state that greets a new account.

**Group name** is the only field, and even it is optional:

> Leave it blank and the group is named after whoever is in it.

So "Goa trip" if you want one, or nothing at all for the flat you share with two
people whose names will do.

### Choose an icon

**Group cover** offers **Choose an icon** — one of the drawn marks: Beach,
Mountains, Camping, Flight, Road trip, Boat, Home, Apartment, Stay, Rent, Bills,
Savings, Meals, Pizza, Takeaway, Coffee, Birthday, Drinks, Party, Gift, Couple,
Sport, Favourite, Friends.

A photo from your phone is also possible, but:

> Group photos are a Plus feature. Pick an icon, or upgrade to add a photo.

The icons are free, there are two dozen of them, and they translate — a photo
does not.

### What kind of group?

Under **More options** (_Type, dates, budget_) there is **Kind**: **Trip**,
**Home**, **Couple**, **Event**, **Friends**, **Other**.

The kind is mostly a label, with one exception. A **Trip** can carry dates and a
budget, and once it has dates the app offers day-by-day reminders and the trip
tools — **Places**, spending by day, and the travel split presets described in
[How splitting works](../using-waves/how-splitting-works.md#trip-presets). If
you skip it at the start, the group asks once:

> **Want to plan this trip?** Add dates to turn on daily reminders, or set a
> budget to track spending.

**Trip budget** is optional either way. The dates and budget rows appear only on
a Trip; afterwards the budget is edited on the trip's **Plan** screen, which the
**•••** menu offers for trips and nothing else.

### Start from an existing group

**Start from an existing group** copies the people and settings out of a group
you already have, so the second Goa trip does not mean adding the same five
people again. You can rename it, change the icon, and drop anyone before it is
made.

## Adding people

Two kinds of people go into a group:

- **People with Waves.** They join through a link, or you find them by their
  exact email or phone number.
- **People without it.** You type a name and they exist in the group as a
  placeholder holding real balances. They never need to install anything.

  > They do not need the app. Add them now and they can claim their history
  > later.

The second kind is the common case, and it is worth understanding before you
lean on it — see
[People and placeholders](../using-waves/people-and-placeholders.md).

**Add from contacts** copies a name out of your address book. Your contacts are
not uploaded; the picker runs on the phone.

## Inviting them

**Invite people** in the group's **•••** menu gives you one link that anybody can
use to join:

> Anyone with this link can join {group}, so share it with people you trust.

You can send it through **WhatsApp**, **Copy link**, or **Share another way**,
and there is a **Scan to join** QR code on the same screen for when the other
person is sitting next to you. The message it sends reads:

> Join {group} on Waves to split expenses — no app or account needed to start.

The link keeps working; it is not a one-shot code. A group admin can reset it if
it ends up somewhere it should not be.

## Leaving, archiving, deleting

Three different things, in the group's **•••** menu:

| Action      | What it does                          | Who is affected            |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **Archive** | _Off your list; nothing is deleted._  | You (anyone can unarchive) |
| **Leave**   | _You step out, the group carries on._ | You                        |
| **Delete**  | _Gone for everyone, with no undo._    | Everybody                  |

You cannot leave a group you still have a balance in:

> You still have a balance in this group. Leaving now would strand it — settle
> up, then leave.

Deleting an unsettled group warns harder, because it destroys somebody else's
record too:

> Deleting it wipes that record for everyone in the group, not just for you.
> Nobody will be able to look up who owed what.

Only a group admin can delete a group. Archived groups are listed under
**Home → ••• → Archived groups** and come back with **Unarchive**. A group left
untouched for eighteen months archives itself; nothing is deleted, and you can
reopen it whenever you like.
