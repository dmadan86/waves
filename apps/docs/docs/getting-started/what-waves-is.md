Waves records who paid for what, works out who owes whom, and keeps that record
straight until everybody is square. It never moves money. When you settle up,
you pay each other however you already do — cash, UPI, a bank transfer — and
Waves writes down that it happened.

## The one idea

**Money belongs to a group.** Not to a person, and not to a running total.

A debt in Waves is always about something — a trip, a flat, a dinner — so before
you can record an expense there has to be a group to put it in. The app says as
much when you have none yet:

> You have no groups yet. A person belongs to a group in Waves, because a debt
> is always about something — a trip, a flat, a dinner.

If somebody just owes you for one thing and a whole group feels like overkill,
**Add a person** on the Friends tab makes the smallest possible one for you. See
[People and placeholders](../using-waves/people-and-placeholders.md).

## The four tabs

Along the bottom of the app there are four destinations and a mic.

| Tab          | What lives there                                                                         |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Home**     | Your groups, your overall balance, and the **+** that starts a new expense.              |
| **Friends**  | People rather than groups: who owes you, who you owe, across everything.                 |
| **Activity** | Every expense, edit, deletion and settlement, for everyone in your groups.               |
| **Personal** | Your own money — solo spending, income, recurring bills, loans, budgets. Private to you. |

The raised button in the middle is the mic: **Speak an expense**. Tap it and say
what you spent — "dinner 800, split with Ravi" — and Waves fills in the form for
you to check. It listens on the device.

There is no Settings tab. Settings live behind your photo in the Home header,
and behind the **•••** menu on Home, which holds **Your account**,
**Notifications**, **Archived groups**, **Language**, **Appearance**,
**Settings** and **Take the tour again**.

## What it does without a signal

Everything you enter is written to your phone first, straight away, and sent to
the server when there is a connection. The app's own words for it:

> Saved on this phone straight away, with or without a signal. The server
> recomputes every share before it is stored, so no device can push a wrong
> number into the ledger.

So a train tunnel is not a problem, and neither is a phone that has been offline
for a week. What it does mean is that two people editing the same expense at
opposite ends of a tunnel can both be right; the server settles it when they
reconnect, and the [activity feed](../using-waves/balances.md#the-activity-feed)
shows what changed.

## What costs money

The ledger does not, and is not meant to. The app's own upgrade screen puts it
this way about groups, expenses, splits, balances, settling up and getting all
of it back out again:

> A ledger you can only half read is not a ledger.

There is a paid tier called **Plus**, and today there is nothing to buy:

> **Nothing to buy yet.** This is the door, not the shop. When there is
> something worth paying for it will be here, with the price on it and no
> surprises.

Plus is currently granted only by a redeem code — **Settings → Redeem a code** —
which is handed out by hand, "for a support case, a thank-you, or a trial".

Four things are capped on the free plan, and each says so where you meet it:

| Cap               | Where you meet it                                                                                                  |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Group photo**   | Group cover. The two dozen drawn icons are free; a photo is Plus.                                                  |
| **Receipts**      | A per-group limit on scanned bills. See [Receipts](../using-waves/receipts.md#limits-on-the-free-plan).            |
| **Cloud storage** | A total size for photos and receipts, shown under **Storage usage**.                                               |
| **Devices**       | _"The free plan covers two devices at a time. A device you have not opened in a while stops counting on its own."_ |

If you are signed in on a third device, the app asks you to clear one rather
than locking you out silently: _"Log out the others to keep using Waves on this
one."_ **Settings → Devices** shows where you are signed in.

## What it never does

- **It never moves money.** Every settlement is a record of a payment you made
  some other way. The app labels it: _Recorded, not moved by Waves._
- **It never uploads your contacts.** Adding somebody from your address book
  copies one name across; the book stays on your phone.
- **It never overwrites history.** Editing an expense keeps the old version, and
  a deleted expense can be brought back for 30 days.
