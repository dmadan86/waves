# Waves help

Waves keeps track of shared money — a trip, a flat, a dinner, a person who owes
you for a taxi. This is the manual for it: what each screen does, what the words
on it mean, and what happens to your data.

If you are opening the app for the first time, start with
[What Waves is](./getting-started/what-waves-is.md) and work down the sidebar.
If you are looking for one specific thing, the sections below jump straight to
it.

## Get going

- [What Waves is](./getting-started/what-waves-is.md) — the four screens, and the one idea underneath them.
- [Create your account](./getting-started/create-your-account.md) — sign in with Apple, Google, a phone number, an email, or nothing at all.
- [Your first group](./getting-started/your-first-group.md) — make a group, add people, invite them.

## Day to day

- [Common ways to use Waves](./using-waves/common-ways-to-use-waves.md) — where to start if you are setting up a group, riding along, travelling, or tracking what you paid.
- [Add an expense](./using-waves/add-an-expense.md) — by hand, by voice, or by scanning the bill.
- [How splitting works](./using-waves/how-splitting-works.md) — Equally, Exact, Shares, Percent, and what happens to the odd rupee.
- [People and placeholders](./using-waves/people-and-placeholders.md) — adding somebody who does not have the app.
- [Balances](./using-waves/balances.md) — what "you lent" and "you borrowed" mean on a row.
- [Settle up](./using-waves/settle-up.md) — recording a payment, and confirming one.
- [Receipts](./using-waves/receipts.md) — scanning, attaching, keeping and sharing a bill.
- [Personal](./using-waves/personal-ledger.md) — your own money, private to you.

## Your data

- [How people find you](./privacy/discovery-settings.md) — who can look you up, and what group-mates see.
- [Export your data](./privacy/export-your-data.md) — a full, lossless copy, free, at any time.
- [Delete your account](./privacy/delete-your-account.md) — what goes, what stays, and why.

There is a developer API. It is new, and the reference is generated from its
OpenAPI document — start at the [API overview](./api/overview.md).
