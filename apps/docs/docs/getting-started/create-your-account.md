Waves opens on **Create account**. If you have used it before, the link at the
bottom takes you to **Welcome back** instead. The two are separate pages, and
they do not offer quite the same things.

## Ways to sign in

On both pages:

- **Continue with Apple** — on iPhone and iPad.
- **Continue with Google**.
- **Continue with phone** — you get a code by SMS and type it in.
- **Continue with email** — either a password, or **Email me a code**, which
  sends a six-digit code instead.

On **Welcome back** there is also **Forgot password**.

Whichever you pick becomes the way back into this account, so pick one you will
still have on your next phone.

## Starting without an account

**Continue as guest** is on the **Create account** page only. It makes a real
account that lives on this phone, with no email and no password, so you can
split a bill before deciding whether you want Waves at all.

A guest has two limits, and the app counts them out loud:

- **One group.** A guest may be in exactly one group — one they started, or one
  they were invited to.
- **Ten days of writing.** After that the app turns read-only: everything is
  still there and still readable, but nothing new can be added until you attach
  an email, a phone number or a provider.

The banner says it plainly: _"{days} days left as a guest — sign up to keep
going after that."_ and, once the window closes, _"Your guest trial has ended —
the app is read-only. Sign up to keep adding."_

:::warning Do not sign out of a guest account

A guest account has no email and no password, so there is nothing to sign back
in **with**. Signing out ends it. Add a way to sign in first — **Add your
details**, on the banner or in **Your account** — and the app says why:

> Add a way to sign in, so this account is still yours on your next phone.

:::

## Signing up later keeps everything

Attaching an email or a phone number to a guest account **upgrades it in
place**. It does not make a second account and it does not copy anything across:

> Everything you have already added stays exactly where it is. This only adds a
> way to sign back in.

Your groups, expenses, balances and settlements all stay put, along with your
place in anybody else's group.

## Your account details

**Home → ••• → Your account** holds the rest:

- **Display name** and **Your photo** — what group-mates see.
- **Region**: your country, which sets your **Currency** and decides which
  payment options you are offered.
- **How people pay you** — a UPI ID or the equivalent for your country. This is
  shown to somebody settling with you so they can pay you from their own bank
  app. _Waves never handles the money._
- **Address** — optional, and only if you want it stored.

Language, Appearance and the privacy controls live beside it; see
[How people find you](../privacy/discovery-settings.md).
