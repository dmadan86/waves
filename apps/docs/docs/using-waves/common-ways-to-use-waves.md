Different people open Waves for different jobs. The same ledger supports all of
them, but the fastest path through the app depends on what you are trying to do.

## I am setting up the group

Start with [Your first group](../getting-started/your-first-group.md). As the
person setting up a trip, flat or dinner group, your job is to:

1. create the group;
2. add people or placeholders;
3. invite anyone who wants to join with their own account;
4. decide whether receipts are visible to everybody or only to the people on the
   expense.

A placeholder is safe to use while somebody is not on Waves yet: their balance is
real, and they can claim it later. If the group is over, archive it. If an admin
deletes it while it is unsettled, Waves warns that the record disappears for
everybody, not only for the admin.

## I am in the split, but I did not pay

Use [How splitting works](./how-splitting-works.md) to check why you owe what you
owe. On an expense, **you borrowed** means somebody else paid and your share is
included. **Not involved** means you are looking at the group record, but that
bill does not touch your balance.

If something is wrong, edit the expense instead of settling around the mistake.
Settling records a payment; it does not fix the original split.

## I am travelling or adding receipts on the go

Read [Receipts](./receipts.md) and [Add an expense](./add-an-expense.md). The
important travel rules are:

- the receipt photo is written to your phone first;
- uploads keep trying when the connection comes back;
- a private receipt can be limited to the people on that expense;
- currencies stay separate in balances, even if a group stores rates for
  individual expenses.

So you can scan a bill with weak signal, keep the photo visible while it uploads,
and assign it to the right group later. Always check the scanned total before
saving; entering the amount by hand is always free.

## I paid and want to get reimbursed

Use [Balances](./balances.md) to see who owes whom, then
[Settle up](./settle-up.md) to record the payment when it happens outside Waves.
Waves never moves money. It can open UPI or another payment app with the handle
filled in, but the payment itself is between you and the other person.

A settlement is pending until the other side confirms it. If you recorded the
wrong payment, cancel it. If somebody says they paid you and the money did not
arrive, reject it; that removes the pending settlement without changing the
balance.

## I only want to track my own money

Use [Personal](./personal-ledger.md). Personal entries are private to you and do
not affect group balances. Use it for solo spending, income, budgets, loans and
recurring bills. Use a group when somebody else also relies on the record.

## I am worried about privacy

Start with [How people find you](../privacy/discovery-settings.md). You decide
whether people can find you by exact email or phone, and whether people already
in your groups can see your contact details. Turning discovery off never changes
who owes what.

For a full copy of your record, use [Export your data](../privacy/export-your-data.md).
If you delete your account, your personal details go, but shared expenses and
settlements remain because they are also other people's records.
