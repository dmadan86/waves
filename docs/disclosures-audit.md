# Disclosures audit: ads and data, code vs. what we say

Audit date 2026-11-17, against `main` at `62fe30ba`. Read-only: nothing else was changed.
Every fact cites `file:line`. "Code default" means the value in the repo's migrations or
constants; **production flag and settings values were not read** (see Open questions).

Headline: the website copy and privacy policy were updated for banner ads; the Play listing
doc, the in-app privacy text and one paragraph of the privacy policy still describe the old
"optional reward ads only" world. No surface mentions the full-screen ad after saving a group
expense. The Play Data safety table says nothing is shared, voice is not collected and
analytics is opt-in; all three are wrong.

### 1.5 Ad gates (export and extra groups)

- Free groups: `FREE_GROUPS = 2`. The gate applies when the person has already created 2 groups, so the **first two** are free; every export is gated (`apps/mobile/src/lib/adPacingPure.ts:110-124`). `docs/ads-pacing.md:19` and the website still say "first".
- A gated action costs 6 credits or one rewarded ad; credits are spent first (`apps/mobile/src/lib/adGateEngine.ts:71-76`; `packages/db/prisma/migrations/20261029120000_credits/migration.sql:18-23`: 6 credits per ad, export 6, group 6, wallet cap 30).
- Only a completed ad unlocks (`adPacingPure.ts:105-108`). Exceptions that let the action through: gate status unreadable (`apps/mobile/src/lib/adGates.ts:131-134`), three no-fills in a row (`adGateEngine.ts:94-116`), two offline passes per 7 days (`adGateEngine.ts:10-11`, `:74-75`), any unexpected error (`apps/mobile/src/lib/useAdGate.tsx:177-186`).

## 5. Open questions the code cannot settle

1. **Production flags.** What are `enabled` and `rollout_percent` for `ads_gates`, `ads_native` and `ads_rewarded`, and who is on `ads_allowlist`? Code defaults are off, 0%. Check with `select key, enabled, rollout_percent from feature_flags where key like 'ads_%';`.
2. **Production settings.** The single `ad_reward_settings` row (caps, grace, `banner_min_account_age_days`, `interstitial_video_enabled`, `placements` jsonb) can differ from every default quoted here.
3. **AdMob console.** Is the EEA/UK/CH consent message published, are US-state messages on, is mediation or any third-party demand enabled, and are the sensitive categories blocked? `docs/ads.md:139-154` is an unchecked list.
4. **Microsoft Clarity.** Is the project's masking set to Strict (`apps/mobile/src/lib/clarity.ts:18-22`), and do Clarity's terms make Microsoft a processor or an independent user of the recordings? That decides whether App interactions is also "Shared" for Clarity and whether privacy page line 93 stands.
5. **User IDs shared with AdMob.** The SSV account id is an opaque UUID used to verify rewards. The sheet marks it Shared to be safe; a lawyer may accept "not shared".
6. **Which build is on Play.** Do the Play binaries carry the real AdMob app ids and all five unit ids, and is `AD_ID` in the merged manifest of the uploaded bundle? The repo's local manifest does not show merged library permissions.
7. **Is `rewardedAds.footnote` shown anywhere?** No usage was found; if dead, delete it rather than translate it.
8. **Videos.** Whether trip albums accept video was not checked; the sheet says Videos: No.
9. **iOS.** `en.json:1299` says iPhone is coming soon. The Apple label mapping in 4.3 is a starting point for when it ships.
10. **Email and device-encryption claims** ("no tracking pixels", "encrypted at rest") were not verified in this audit.
