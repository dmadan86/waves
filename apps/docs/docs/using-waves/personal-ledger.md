**Personal** is the fourth tab. Everything in it is yours alone:

> Your own money — private to you.

Nothing here is shared with a group, nobody in your groups can see it, and it
never touches a balance. Entries carry the reminder on screen: _Private to you ·
Not shared with groups._

It is the answer to "I want to track my own spending too, and I do not want a
second app for it".

## This month

The top of the screen is one month at a time — **Previous month** and **Next
month** step through — with **Income**, **Expenses** and **Net** for it. Net is
labelled honestly in both directions: **saved** or **overspent**, plus a
**Savings rate**.

Underneath, a one-line comparison: _You spent {amount} more than last month_, or
less, or _About the same spend as last month_. **Where your money went** breaks
the month down by category, and **Last 3 months** puts it in context.

## Adding an entry

**Add** offers **Add expense** and **Add income**. Both ask for an **Amount**, a
**Note** (_What was it for?_), a **Date** and a **Category**.

Income also asks for a **Source**, from a built-in list: Salary, Business,
Freelance, Rent received, Interest, Dividends, Investment sale, Pension, Bonus,
Commission, Royalties, Refund, Gift, Benefit, Other income.

Categories and sources are the same ones the shared side uses, including any you
made yourself under **Tags & categories**. **Category packs** add ready-made
sets — _"Sets of categories and income sources, ready to add to your own list"_.

## Tools

Three tools sit under the month.

### Recurring

_Bills and income that repeat._

A recurring item has an amount, a category, and a repeat: **Weekly**,
**Fortnightly**, **Twice a month**, **Monthly**, **Every few months**,
**Quarterly**, **Half-yearly**, **Yearly**. It can have a start date, an end
date, or **No end**.

**Add automatically** decides what happens when one falls due:

> Post the entry on its own when due. Off just reminds you.

With it off, the item appears under **Due** and **Add now** records it. **Every
month** lists each occurrence so you can mark one **Received** or **Paid**, or
see one **Missed**.

### Loans

_Money you owe or are owed._

A loan is **I borrowed** or **I lent**, with a counterpart (_A name — a friend,
a bank, anyone_), an amount, and an **Outstanding** figure that comes down as
you **Record payment**. **Close** finishes it; **Reopen** brings it back.

This is separate from what a group-mate owes you. A loan here is your own
record, between you and anybody — including an institution that will never be in
a group.

### Budgets

_Monthly caps by category._

A budget is a **Monthly limit** on one category, or **Overall** for everything.
The screen shows **Spent** against it and how much is **left**, or how far
**over**. Anything past its cap is flagged **Over budget**.

## Not the same as "Just for me"

Two things sound alike and are not.

**Just for me**, on the shared add-expense screen, takes a spend off the group's
ledger and parks it in your own **Saved for later**:

> Not splitting this? Keep it in your own captures — off the group's ledger.

That is a holding pen. It is still an expense waiting for a group, and it can
still be assigned to one.

**Personal** is not a holding pen. Nothing here is waiting to become a shared
expense, and nothing here is ever promoted into a group by accident. Moving
money between the two sides is always something you do deliberately.

## Backing it up

Personal records can be copied to your own Google Drive and locked with a key
only you hold:

> ...locked with a key only you hold. Neither Waves nor Google can read it.

The key is 64 characters, generated on your phone, and the app is blunt about
what that means:

> Write it down: it is the only way to open the backup on a new phone, and
> nobody can give it back to you — not Waves, not Google.

Automatic backup can run **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**, over **Wi-Fi
only** or **Wi-Fi or mobile data**. Backups run when you open the app, not while
it is closed. **Restore** brings records back and never overwrites what is
already on the phone.
