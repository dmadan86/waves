# Competitor research for the /compare pages

Checked 2026-10-25. Rule used by the pages: a competitor fact appears only if it is in a table below with a source. Anything marked **unverified** is left off the pages or shown as "Not listed in the sources we checked". Prices are store prices and vary by country.

Method: live web research (WebFetch/WebSearch) plus an independent Codex pass with web search; the second table block (from Codex) was spot-checked against the live pages for Kittysplit pricing, Tricount's FAQ, Splitwise's limit article and Splitkaro's Premium FAQ, all of which matched.

## Manual way (spreadsheets, WhatsApp notes)

General properties, no competitor-specific claims: a sheet or a chat note does not compute who owes whom unless you build the formulas, does not keep per-person history, and has no settle-up hand-off. These are described as "what you build yourself" rather than as shortcomings of a named product.

## Dropped / not covered

- No named app was found to be discontinued. SpendSync, Gully, yelepaise (India) were found but are early or unverified, so not given pages. Regional candidates for later: Revolut Group Bills (UK), Beem (AU), Ziina (UAE), Splyt (US) - sources below.
- Tricount Premium and in-app export were discontinued (stated on the Tricount page).
