**Settings → Export data**. There is no paywall on it and there never has been:
_Everything, always free._

## The three formats

| Format                | What it is                                                           |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **JSON (lossless)**   | Everything, in full fidelity. Enough to rebuild your ledger exactly. |
| **CSV (spreadsheet)** | The spreadsheet view, including per-person settlement detail.        |
| **PDF (printable)**   | A readable statement.                                                |

What "lossless" means here, in the app's words:

> JSON includes every version of every expense, who paid, who owed, settlements
> with their per-expense allocations, and the activity trail — enough to rebuild
> your ledger exactly.

That is the important promise. It is not a summary and not a snapshot of current
balances — it is the history, including the versions of an expense that were
edited away.

## What to export

**All my groups**, or a single group. A single-group export can also be produced
from the group itself, under **••• → Export**:

> A tidy statement of this group — balances, every expense and settlement — as a
> PDF to read or an Excel workbook to crunch. Built on your device from what you
> already have, so it works offline.

The group export comes as a **PDF** statement or an **Excel** workbook with a
sheet each for Summary, Expenses, Settlements, Balances and Members.

## Getting the file

Tap **Export**, wait for **Export ready**, and the share sheet opens so you can
send it wherever you keep things — email, Drive, a file manager.

On the web app the file is written to the app cache instead; _"use a device to
share it onward."_

## Bringing data in

The same screen has **Import your data** for the other direction. Waves can read
a Splitwise export and rebuild the group's history from it, and can read bank
SMS messages on the phone to suggest expenses — read on the device, and confirmed
by you before anything is recorded.

## Why this exists

An export you cannot get out is not a record, it is a hostage. Waves keeps
shared financial history that other people rely on, so the copy has to be
complete, free, and available without asking anybody.

If you are moving to your own servers rather than to another app, the
self-hosting guide in
[MIGRATION.md]({{repoUrl}}/blob/main/MIGRATION.md) covers the whole stack rather
than one account's data.
